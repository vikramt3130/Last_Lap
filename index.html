HOW TO
STAY IN.

01

Build speed. You run automatically; pace starts low and gradually rises. Hold A/D or ←/→ to adjust speed.

02

Read the track. Gap widths and hook positions change each round. Some lip-hook gaps have a deeper backup; other gaps have only one hook. Striped walls follow the landing runway; press Space to vault them. Slide under the low beam after the wall for a speed boost.

03

Use your hook. Tap W or ↑ when a pit hook lights up. On lip-hook gaps, an orange backup may appear deeper in the pit.

04

Slide to stay low. Hold S or ↓ to slide under the low beams. Fall off the course and you're out.

05

Win the set. The round continues until every racer is out; the last racer eliminated earns 10 points, then 4, 2, and 1 by placement. Every 3 seconds survived adds 1 bonus point. Highest score after 3 rounds wins.
